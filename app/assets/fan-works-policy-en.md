## Our position

- Aipictors does not ban fan works (works based on existing titles or characters) across the board.
- We also do not turn off support (coins) or Creator Rewards for a work just because it is a fan work.
- Whether fan works are allowed, and whether you may earn money from them, is decided by each rights holder (the original author, publisher, game company and so on). **Checking and following the rights holder's guidelines and terms is the poster's responsibility.**
- Aipictors does not decide or guarantee whether fan works or earnings are allowed for any particular title.

## For creators

- Before posting a fan work, check the fan work guidelines on the original title's official site.
- If the guidelines forbid paid use, tips or donations, please do not accept support for that work. Turn on "Do not accept support" under "Receiving support" in Settings to stop receiving support.
- If a title has no public guidelines, it is unclear whether earning from it is allowed. Please decide carefully.
- These points are set by our terms:
  - Fan fiction (novels) may only be posted when the rights holder has publicly stated their view on fan works ([Terms of Service](/terms)).
  - Posting other people's works without permission, or editing them with i2i, ControlNet and similar tools, is prohibited ([Terms of Service](/terms)).
  - Paid requests may not ask for, or deliver, copies of other people's works or characters ([Paid Request Terms](/requests/terms)).
- When a rights holder files a complaint, we act under our terms, for example by making the work private or removing it.

## For viewers and supporters

- Some people enjoy fan works, some enjoy only originals, some use support and some do not. We respect every way of enjoying the site. You are never expected to join in supporting each other or to share any particular view.
- Please do not attack or call out other people's works in comments or on social media because they earn money. If you think a work infringes someone's rights, tell us with "Report a problem" on the work page.
- You can keep things you don't want to see off your screen:
  - **Muted tags** ([Settings → Mute → Tags](/settings/muted/tags)): hides works with a title, character or genre tag.
  - **Hide support and support rankings** (Settings → Receiving support): hides support buttons, support counts, support rankings and support notifications on your screen.
  - **"Originals only" on Home**: shows only works tagged "オリジナル" (original), "オリキャラ" (original character) or "創作" (original creation) in Home lists.
