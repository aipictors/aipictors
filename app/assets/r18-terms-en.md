# R18 Content Terms (Posting Guidelines)

Last updated: September 2026

This page defines additional rules for posting and publishing R18/R18G content on Aipictors. These rules apply in addition to the general Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

## 1. Scope

- These rules apply to content with sexual expression, graphic violence, or highly intense material.
- If something is not defined here, the standard Terms and Community Guidelines apply.
- For community safety, moderation decisions may consider context, visibility, and how content is presented.

## 2. Rating Selection (Required)

When posting, choose the correct rating for the content.

- R18: Content with sexual expression
- R18G: Content with stronger violent, brutal, or graphic elements

The following may be removed or prohibited regardless of selected rating:

- Sexual content involving minors or minor-looking characters
- Non-consensual sexual deepfakes of real people
- Direct encouragement of crime, hate, or self-harm/violence

## 3. Presentation Requirements

Please consider safety not only in the work itself, but also in title, tags, description, and thumbnail.

- Thumbnail: avoid highly explicit visuals in public listing contexts
- Title/description: avoid overtly explicit sexual or violent wording
- Tags: use accurate and non-misleading tags

## 4. Concealment Requirements (Mosaic, Masking, etc.)

Use concealment strong enough to prevent clear recognition for scenes such as:

- Explicit depiction of genitals and penetration areas
- Severe gore (e.g., exposed organs, highly graphic injury details)

Examples of acceptable methods:

- Mosaic
- Solid fill masking
- Shape/effect overlays that fully block details

Notes:

- If details become recognizable on zoom, concealment may be judged insufficient.
- Partial blur or translucent masking may be rejected.

## 5. Prohibited or Strictly Reviewed Content

The following is prohibited or subject to strict review:

- Child sexual exploitation context, including near-equivalent depictions
- Explicit glorification of non-consensual or coercive sexual violence
- Extreme brutality that can be mistaken for real harm
- Praise or recruitment of hate, discrimination, or illegal acts
- Content violating applicable law, platform policy, or payment/distribution rules

## 6. Comments, Stamps, and Derivative Posts

These rules also apply to comments, stamps, quote posts, and derivative edits around R18 content.

- Harassment, threats, and doxxing are prohibited.
- Even if the original post is compliant, violations in comments can be moderated separately.

## 7. Age Eligibility and Viewing Settings

- Minors may not view or post R18-targeted content.
- Users are responsible for managing their own viewing settings.
- False age declarations or misuse of viewing settings may lead to restrictions.

## 8. Enforcement

Depending on severity, Aipictors may take actions such as:

- Requesting rating corrections
- Hiding or deleting posts
- Limiting features (posting, commenting, generation, etc.)
- Suspending or terminating accounts

For severe or repeated violations, immediate action may be taken without prior notice.

## 9. Reporting and Revisions

- Please report suspected violations through the reporting function.
- These rules may be revised to reflect legal and operational updates.

When updated, the revision date on this page will be changed.
