# R18 Content Terms

Last updated: October 27, 2026

These terms apply in addition to the [Terms of Service](/terms) when you view, post or generate R-18 / R-18G works on Aipictors. Where they overlap, the stricter rule applies. The Japanese version is authoritative.

## 1. Age

- R-18 / R-18G works may only be viewed, posted or generated by people **aged 18 or older**.
- If you are under 18, do not switch to R18. Accounts found to misrepresent their age will be restricted.
- You can choose what you see with the "All ages / R18" switch in the header and in [Settings > Content](/settings/restriction).

## 2. Content rating (required)

Set the correct rating when posting. If unsure, choose the stricter one.

| Rating | Guideline |
| --- | --- |
| All ages | No sexual or violent content |
| R-15 | Mild sexual content (underwear, swimwear, revealing outfits, suggestive poses) |
| R-18 | Sexual acts, exposed breasts or genitals |
| R-18G | Strong gore such as blood, mutilation or cruelty |

## 3. Censoring (mosaic etc.)

As required by Japanese law, the following must be censored so they cannot be made out, for **both illustrations and realistic images**. Uncensored works may not be posted or generated.

- Genitals and anything resembling them
- Genital or anal penetration and contact

Use a mosaic, solid fill or an opaque shape. Light blur, translucent overlays or insufficient automatic censoring may not be accepted.

## 4. Child protection (strict)

Regardless of rating, the following are prohibited and will lead to removal and account suspension:

- Anything that may be mistaken for child sexual abuse material, especially **realistic** sexual images of people who look under 18
- Realistic sexual images that include school uniforms, school bags, school backgrounds and similar
- Child-like bodies with heavy skin exposure

If you find such content, do not save it and report it.

## 5. Other prohibited content

- Sexual images or non-consensual deepfakes of real people
- Content glorifying non-consensual sexual violence, or extreme cruelty evoking real incidents
- Hate speech, or content encouraging crime or self-harm
- Content that violates laws or payment/hosting provider rules

## 6. Presentation

- Avoid overly explicit titles, descriptions and tags, and use accurate tags.
- Profile and header images must not contain sexual content.
- These terms also apply to comments and stickers on R18 works.

## 7. R18 sub-account

You may create one sub-account for R18. Image generation cannot be used on it, and do not like the same work from both accounts.

## 8. Image generation

All generated images are reviewed, including deleted ones. Generating uncensored images or images that may be mistaken for child sexual abuse material will lead to suspension of the generation feature.

## 9. Enforcement and reports

Depending on severity we may change the rating, unpublish or delete works, restrict features or suspend the account, sometimes without prior notice. Report suspected violations from the work page, and contact us via [Support](/support/chat) if you disagree with a decision.
