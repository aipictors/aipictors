# Premium Coin Policy and Prepaid Payment Instrument Notice

Last updated: June 16, 2026

This page summarizes the usage rules, expiration, refund handling, exchange conditions, and prepaid payment instrument disclosures related to Premium Coins on Aipictors.

## 1. Overview

- Premium Coins are paid digital coins provided for use within Aipictors.
- They may be used for image generation, user support, and other eligible features designated by Aipictors.
- Premium Coins are managed in one-coin units and may only be used within the service.
- Purchases for users in Japan are processed through Stripe or other designated payment processors.

## 2. Scope of Use

- Premium Coins may only be used for services and features designated by Aipictors.
- They are not cash, bank deposits, e-money, or any other legal tender substitute.
- Except where required by law or expressly permitted by Aipictors, they may not be transferred, loaned, pledged, or redeemed for cash.

## 3. Expiration

- Purchased Premium Coins expire three months after the purchase date.
- Any unused Premium Coins may expire automatically after the validity period ends.
- Expired Premium Coins are generally not reissued, refunded, or compensated.

## 4. Relationship to Support Coins

- Premium Coins received from other users as support may be tracked separately from purchased Premium Coins.
- Only Premium Coins that Aipictors designates as exchange-eligible support coins may be used to request Amazon gift card exchange.
- Purchased Premium Coins are generally not eligible for such exchange.

## 5. Pricing and Contract Formation

- Prices, coin quantities, bonus quantities, and minimum purchase requirements are shown on the purchase screen or related pages.
- The purchase contract is deemed formed when payment is completed and Aipictors or its payment processor confirms the transaction.
- Reflection in the user balance may take time due to network or system conditions.

## 6. Cancellation and Refunds

- Due to the nature of digital content, purchases are generally non-cancellable and non-refundable unless required by law or caused by Aipictors.
- If duplicate charges, system errors, or similar issues occur, Aipictors may investigate and provide corrective action or refunds on a case-by-case basis.
- If fraud, chargebacks, or terms violations are detected, Aipictors may revoke balances, suspend usage, or restrict the account.

## 7. Prohibited Conduct

- Obtaining or using Premium Coins through unauthorized means
- Using bots, reverse engineering, exploits, or unauthorized access methods
- Manipulating support, rankings, or exchange requests through multi-account abuse or other fraudulent conduct
- Any use in violation of law, the terms of service, or public order and morality
